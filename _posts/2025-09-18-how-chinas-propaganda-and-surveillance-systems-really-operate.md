### How China’s Surveillance and Propaganda Tech Mimics Global Biz Leaked docs from Chinese firms like Geedge and GoLaxy expose how they build and sell advanced tools for monitoring internet traffic, censoring content, and deploying AI-driven propaganda—stuff that’s essentially authoritarian tech-as-a-service. These companies partner with academics, repurpose rival gear, and tailor solutions for governments, mirroring how Western startups commercialize research. For SMBs and MSPs, this underscores the blurred lines between everyday tech and surveillance risks, as similar tools could infiltrate your supply chain or expose data to foreign entities. It’s a wake-up call to scrutinize tech partners, since what starts as efficient software might end up as a backdoor for spying. Plus, understanding these operations helps you spot parallels in your own operations, avoiding costly pitfalls like unintended compliance issues or security breaches. Takeaways: - Audit tech suppliers for surveillance risks.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Strengthen defenses against traffic interception tools.
- Evaluate hidden costs of dual-use software.
- Monitor global export trends for compliance threats.
