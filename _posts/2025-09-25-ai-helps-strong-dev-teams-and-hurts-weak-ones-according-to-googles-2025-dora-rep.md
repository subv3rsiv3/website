![AI helps strong dev teams and hurts weak ones, according to Google's 2025 DORA report](/assets/images/ai-helps-strong-dev-teams-and-hurts-weak-ones-according-to-googles-2025-dora-rep-hero.webp)
Google's 2025 DORA report reveals that AI is a double-edged sword for software dev teams, supercharging the efficient ones while exposing and exacerbating the chaos in underperforming outfits—think of it as giving a sports car to a pro driver versus a reckless teen. Nearly all developers are now leaning on AI tools for at least a couple hours daily, boosting productivity for 80% of them, but only if they've got solid foundations like robust version control and quality platforms in place. The report identifies key practices, such as building strong internal data ecosystems and adopting value stream management, as essential for turning AI into a real asset rather than a liability. For SMBs and MSPs, this means ditching the hype and focusing on organizational basics first—otherwise, you're just inviting more bugs and burnout into your workflow. Meanwhile, top teams are debunking the speed-versus-quality myth, delivering fast and stable code by treating AI as an amplifier for their already-smart processes.
