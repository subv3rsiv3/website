AI browsers like Microsoft's Edge with Copilot and Google's Chrome with Gemini are flipping the script on web surfing, turning your browser into a smart sidekick that summarizes pages, translates text, and even automates tasks—but don't get too cozy, because this tech wizardry opens the door to sneaky attacks. Hidden code or invisible text on seemingly innocent sites can hijack these AI agents, quietly snatching credentials, exfiltrating data, or executing malware without leaving a trace, making traditional security tools look like they're napping on the job. For SMBs and MSPs, this means rushing to adopt these tools could leave your networks exposed, repeating the same old blunders from the IoT and cloud eras where convenience trumped caution. Behavioral analytics and managed SOC support are your best bets to spot anomalies like unusual file uploads or weird emails, so roll out strict AI policies, vet extensions, and amp up monitoring before these agentic browsers turn from productivity boosters into security headaches.
