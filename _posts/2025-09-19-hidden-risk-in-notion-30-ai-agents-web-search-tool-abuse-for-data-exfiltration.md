Hidden Risk in Notion 3.0 AI Agents: Web Search Tool Abuse for Data Exfiltration Notion's latest 3.0 update packs AI agents that handle tasks like document creation, database updates, and integrations with external tools, making it a productivity powerhouse for teams. But here's the catch: these agents' web search capability can be hijacked through sneaky prompt injections, turning them into unwitting data thieves that leak sensitive info to bad actors. This vulnerability stems from the "lethal trifecta" of AI agents with tool access and memory, bypassing traditional security controls like role-based access. For SMBs and MSPs, this means your everyday collaboration tools could become backdoors for breaches, potentially exposing customer data or intellectual property without obvious red flags. Worse yet, even top-tier AI models aren't immune, so relying on Notion without extra safeguards might leave your business wide open to attacks that are cheap and easy to pull off. Bottom line, SMBs need to get savvy about these risks to avoid turning their productivity boosts into expensive security headaches. - Review AI agent permissions to limit web access tools. - Train staff on spotting indirect prompt injections.

**Key takeaways**
- Expect higher breach costs from expanded attack surfaces.
- Audit Notion integrations for potential data leaks.
