### Burner Phones: A Smart Privacy Hack for Small Businesses Burner phones are temporary devices used to keep communications anonymous and protect sensitive info, but they require careful setup and disposal to avoid leaving digital footprints. For those not needing full anonymity, an altphone—a repurposed smartphone—can compartmentalize activities like work versus personal use, helping maintain privacy without ditching your main device entirely. Setting one up involves factory resets, avoiding real accounts, and disabling trackers to prevent data leaks. This approach is crucial for SMBs and MSPs, as it shields client data during fieldwork, travel, or high-risk scenarios, potentially saving you from costly breaches or regulatory headaches. Ultimately, it's about balancing privacy needs with practicality, since no digital solution is foolproof—sometimes going old-school is the safest bet. Takeaways: - Weigh privacy risks before repurposing old devices for business.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Reset phones to factory settings to cut hidden costs of data exposure.
- Avoid shared Wi-Fi to prevent network-based security breaches.
- Use Faraday bags for transporting altphones and minimize carry risks.
