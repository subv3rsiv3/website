Developers are jumping on the "vibe coding" bandwagon, where AI tools like GitHub Copilot churn out code via casual chats, promising a 10x productivity boost—but let's face it, most of this stuff is a hot mess, riddled with security holes, inconsistent patterns, and enough technical debt to make your IT budget weep. As Andrej Karpathy's trend explodes, data from GitClear shows AI-assisted code leads to 41% more churn and shakier security, forcing companies to scramble for specialists who can untangle the "AI spaghetti" before it tanks their projects. Enter the booming cleanup economy, with services like VibeCodeFixers.com matching pros to fix wonky AI-generated code at premium rates, turning what started as LinkedIn memes into a lucrative gig for savvy consultants. For SMBs and MSPs, this means treating AI as a speedy but sloppy intern—use it for quick prototypes, but always budget for human oversight to refactor, secure, and scale that code properly, or risk turning your tech stack into a costly liability. Meanwhile, the real opportunity lies in mastering these cleanup skills, as demand for AI code remediators is skyrocketing, offering a fast track to high-paying roles for tech pros who can bridge the gap.
