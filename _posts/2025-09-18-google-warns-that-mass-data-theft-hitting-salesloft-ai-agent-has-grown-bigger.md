### Google Escalates Warnings on Salesloft AI Breach Google's just dropped a bombshell: the hack on Salesloft's Drift AI chat agent has exposed more than initially feared, with attackers swiping credentials to raid Google Workspace emails. In a swift response, Google nuked the compromised tokens, cut off all integrations between Drift and Workspace, and alerted impacted users to batten down the hatches. This mess started small, pinned to Salesforce ties, but now it's clear other connections are at risk, forcing a broader advisory for all Salesloft Drift customers to assume their auth tokens are toast. For SMBs and MSPs leaning on these tools for sales automation and customer chats, this highlights the perils of third-party integrations—your business data could be next if not properly guarded, potentially leading to downtime, compliance headaches, and lost trust. Worst of all, it underscores how quickly a single weak link can snowball into a full-blown security fiasco, making it crucial for smaller outfits to audit their setups before the next breach hits. Takeaways: - Revoke tokens fast to avoid data leaks.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Watch for suspicious Workspace activity now.
- Budget extra for security audits on integrations.
- Switch to multi-factor auth for all tools.
