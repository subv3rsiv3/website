### Google Escalates Warnings on Salesloft Drift Breach Google has flagged a growing security fiasco with Salesloft's Drift AI chat agent, where hackers exploited stolen credentials to break into Google Workspace email accounts, expanding beyond the initial Salesforce tie-ins. In a swift response, Google yanked the compromised tokens, cut off all integrations between Drift and Workspace, and alerted impacted users to safeguard their data. This mess highlights how interconnected business tools can turn a minor glitch into a full-blown headache, especially for SMBs and MSPs who rely on these platforms for everyday operations without massive IT teams. For small outfits, this underscores the real risks of third-party integrations going rogue, potentially exposing sensitive customer data and disrupting workflows that keep the lights on. Worse yet, if you're an MSP juggling multiple clients on these systems, you might now face urgent audits and trust issues that could hit your bottom line hard. Takeaways: - Check and rotate all API tokens pronto to avoid breaches.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Disable suspect integrations to limit exposure risks.
- Budget for extra security audits after third-party fails.
- Watch for phishing spikes targeting compromised accounts.
