### Google Escalates Warnings on Salesloft AI Breach Google's just dropped a bombshell: a hack on Salesloft's Drift AI chat agent has expanded, with attackers swiping credentials to raid Google Workspace emails. Initially pegged to Salesforce integrations, the breach now threatens any tokens linked to the platform, prompting Google to yank those tokens and kill all Workspace connections while they dig deeper. This means Salesloft users, including SMBs and MSPs who lean on these tools for customer outreach and sales automation, could face widespread exposure of sensitive data like emails and contacts. For small businesses, this underscores the perils of relying on third-party AI integrations without airtight security, potentially leading to downtime, compliance headaches, and trust-eroding data leaks that hit operations hard. Takeaways: - Revoke and rotate all API tokens immediately. - Watch for unauthorized Workspace access risks.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Budget for potential integration downtime costs.
- Audit third-party tools for broader vulnerabilities.
