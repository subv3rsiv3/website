### Google Escalates Warnings on Salesloft AI Breach Google has flagged a growing security mess with Salesloft's Drift AI chat agent, where hackers exploited stolen credentials to break into Google Workspace email accounts. Initially, the issue seemed confined to Salesloft's Salesforce integrations, but new intel from Google's Threat Intelligence Group shows it's affecting other connections too, prompting a full token wipe and integration shutdown. As a result, all Salesloft Drift users are urged to assume their auth tokens are toast and take immediate steps to secure their setups. For SMBs and MSPs relying on these tools for customer interactions and data syncing, this highlights the risks of third-party integrations going rogue, potentially exposing sensitive business info and disrupting operations. Worst-case, it could lead to compliance headaches or lost trust with clients, making robust security audits a no-brainer for smaller outfits juggling tight budgets. Takeaways: - Review and rotate all API tokens pronto to avoid breaches.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Watch for unauthorized Workspace access that could leak data.
- Factor in extra downtime costs for emergency integrations.
- Audit third-party tools regularly to spot weak links.
- Train teams quickly on spotting phishing tied to this incident.
