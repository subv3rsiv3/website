### Subvertec Tech Rundown: Google Escalates Warnings on Salesloft Drift Breach Look, folks, Google's just dropped the hammer: the data theft fiasco with Salesloft's Drift AI chat agent is way bigger than we thought, with hackers snagging credentials to raid Google Workspace emails. Initially pegged as a Salesforce integration hiccup, new intel from Google's Threat Intelligence Group reveals it's spilling over to other Salesloft Drift connections, forcing Google to yank compromised security tokens, kill all integrations with Workspace, and alert affected users—basically treating every auth token tied to Drift as tainted. For SMBs and MSPs relying on these tools for customer chats and sales automation, this is a wake-up call: your email data could be exposed, potentially leading to phishing nightmares or compliance headaches, so audit your integrations pronto before the bad guys exploit any weak links. And hey, Salesloft's still playing catch-up without updating their advice, which is about as helpful as a screen door on a submarine—time to double-check your own security setups to avoid getting caught in the crossfire. **Key Takeaways:** - **Revoke and Review:** Immediately check and revoke any authentication tokens linked to Salesloft Drift if you're an SMB or MSP using Google Workspace, as attackers have already breached email access.
