### Google Expands Alert on Salesloft AI Breach Google's just cranked up the urgency on a security snafu with Salesloft's Drift AI chat agent, revealing that hackers snagged credentials to raid Google Workspace emails beyond just Salesforce ties. Initially downplayed as isolated to Salesforce integrations, the breach now looks wider, forcing Google to yank affected tokens, kill all Drift-Workspace links, and alert impacted users to safeguard their accounts. This mess underscores the risks of third-party AI tools mishandling auth tokens, potentially exposing sensitive business data like customer interactions and sales intel. For SMBs and MSPs leaning on these platforms for efficient outreach, this is a wake-up call—your everyday tools might be leaking secrets, leading to downtime, trust hits, and costly security overhauls to prevent future infiltrations. Oh, and Salesloft hasn't chimed in yet, which is never a good sign for quick fixes. Takeaways: - Revoke and rotate all third-party tokens ASAP.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Audit integrations for hidden vulnerabilities.
- Boost training on credential security basics.
- Expect potential downtime from disabled tools.
