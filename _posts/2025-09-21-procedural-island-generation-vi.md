In CartoKit's procedural island gen finale, we're wrapping up by baking that intricate terrain mesh into a compact, metadata-rich asset that packs in elevation, moisture, biomes, and rivers—making it a breeze for tech pros to repurpose without diving back into the code. The CPU-powered debug renderer churns out diagnostic images of everything from seed points to final triangulations, ensuring cross-platform consistency so your visualizations are dead-on accurate and export-ready. Jump into the interactive egui viewer to tweak parameters like rainfall or elevation hints on the fly, then spit out GLB files, PNGs, or even GIFs with a few clicks—ideal for SMBs and makers who want polished procedural content without the hassle of custom dev work. Performance stays zippy, with the whole shebang finishing in under a second, and its modular setup invites you to hack and extend for your own projects, like spicing up game worlds or simulations.
