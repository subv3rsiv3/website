![4 better ways to protect your business than dreaded (and useless) anti-phishing training](/assets/images/4-better-ways-to-protect-your-business-than-dreaded-and-useless-anti-phishing-tr-hero.webp)
Look, if you're running an SMB or MSP and still banking on those yawn-inducing anti-phishing training sessions that employees breeze through in under a minute, you're basically throwing good money after bad—studies show they barely reduce click rates on fake emails. Instead of wasting time on ineffective videos and quizzes, amp up engagement with interactive workshops or virtual sessions that actually get your team talking about real threats like spear phishing and impersonation tactics. Go for gamification with fun, incentive-driven challenges to make learning stick, but pair it with a solid layered defense: deploy advanced email filters, multi-factor authentication, and endpoint monitoring to catch what humans miss. Finally, ease the pressure by fostering a blame-free culture where employees feel safe reporting phishing attempts, turning your whole operation into a more resilient shield against those sneaky cybercriminals who are getting smarter every day.
