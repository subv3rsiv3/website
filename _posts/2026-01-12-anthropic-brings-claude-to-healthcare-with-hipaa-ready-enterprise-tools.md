Anthropic is rolling out its Claude AI platform to the healthcare sector with HIPAA-compliant Enterprise tools, following OpenAI's playbook for ChatGPT, and it's about time AI tackled the industry's bureaucratic headaches head-on. These new connectors let Claude tap into the CMS Coverage Database to check Medicare rules based on location, streamline prior authorizations, and even look up ICD-10 codes to fix coding errors, slash billing mistakes, and speed up claims processing. For SMBs and MSPs dealing with healthcare clients, this means easier revenue cycle management, better compliance, and less time wrestling with outdated systems that feel straight out of the Stone Age. Plus, features like provider verification and credentialing support could cut claim errors dramatically, freeing up your team to focus on what really matters—growing your business without the regulatory red tape bogging you down.
