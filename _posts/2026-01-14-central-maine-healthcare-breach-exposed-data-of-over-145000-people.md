Central Maine Healthcare just fessed up to a nasty data breach from last year, where hackers lurked in their systems for over two months between March and June, ultimately exposing sensitive info on more than 145,000 folks including patients and staff. This mess involved personal details like names, birthdates, Social Security numbers, and health records, putting everyone at higher risk for phishing scams, identity theft, and financial fraud—yet another wake-up call for SMBs to tighten their cybersecurity game. CMH kicked off notifications right away and wrapped up their investigation in November 2025, offering free credit monitoring and a support line to help affected individuals spot and report shady activity. If you're running a small business or MSP, take this as a reminder to audit your own defenses, like implementing multi-factor auth and regular breach drills, before the bad guys come knocking on your digital door.
