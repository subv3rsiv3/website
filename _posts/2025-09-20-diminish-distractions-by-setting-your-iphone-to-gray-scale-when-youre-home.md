![Diminish Distractions by Setting Your iPhone to Gray Scale When You're Home](/assets/images/diminish-distractions-by-setting-your-iphone-to-gray-scale-when-youre-home-hero.webp)
If you're a small-business owner or tech-savvy pro constantly battling phone distractions at home, here's a clever hack to make your iPhone less tempting without sacrificing its utility on the go: use Apple Shortcuts to automatically switch your device to grayscale mode when you arrive home, turning that colorful siren of notifications into a drab, unappealing monochrome that actually lets you focus on real work or hobbies. This setup is dead simple—no fancy apps required, just built-in iOS tools—and it cleverly balances productivity by reverting to full color as soon as you step out the door. For makers and MSPs juggling endless tasks, this tweak can seriously cut down on mindless scrolling, giving you back precious time for things like strategizing your next project or tinkering in the workshop. To get started, fire up the Shortcuts app, create two quick actions—one to enable grayscale and another to disable it—then link them to location-based automations for arriving and leaving home, and bam, you're good to go.
