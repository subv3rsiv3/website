![OpenAI Teams Up With Oracle and SoftBank to Build 5 New Stargate Data Centers](/assets/images/openai-teams-up-with-oracle-and-softbank-to-build-5-new-stargate-data-centers-hero.webp)
OpenAI is ramping up its AI infrastructure game by partnering with Oracle and SoftBank to construct five new data centers in the US under the Stargate project, pushing total capacity to a whopping 7 gigawatts—think of it as powering a small city's worth of AI crunching. Three of these beasts, located in Texas, New Mexico, and an undisclosed Midwest spot, are teaming up with Oracle to handle the heavy lifting, while the other two in Ohio and Texas get a green boost from SoftBank's solar and battery expertise. This expansion, including a 600-megawatt upgrade to their existing Abilene facility, promises over 25,000 jobs, though let's be real, most of that workforce will be busy building before the lights even flicker on for daily ops. For SMBs and MSPs eyeing the AI boom, this massive push highlights how critical reliable, scalable data centers are becoming—your next big opportunity might involve jumping on board with AI services that demand this kind of powerhouse setup to stay competitive without breaking the bank. And hey, with the US doubling down on AI supremacy to outpace rivals like China, it's a cheeky reminder that if you're not planning for gigawatt-scale demands, you might get left in the digital dust.
