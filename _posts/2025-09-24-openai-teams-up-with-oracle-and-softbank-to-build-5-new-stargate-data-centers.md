OpenAI is ramping up its AI infrastructure game by partnering with Oracle and SoftBank to construct five massive new data centers across the US under the Stargate banner, pushing total capacity to a whopping 7 gigawatts—think of it as powering a small city's worth of AI crunching. Three sites, including spots in Texas and New Mexico plus a mystery Midwest location, are teaming up with Oracle to handle everything from training to inference workloads, while the other two in Ohio and Texas are leveraging SoftBank's SB Energy for solar-backed setups. This expansion, including a 600-megawatt boost to their existing Abilene facility, promises over 25,000 jobs and aims to keep the US ahead in the global AI arms race against rivals like China. For SMBs and MSPs, this means more opportunities to tap into scalable cloud resources, but watch out for the skyrocketing energy demands that could drive up costs and complicate your own tech stacks if you're not planning ahead.
