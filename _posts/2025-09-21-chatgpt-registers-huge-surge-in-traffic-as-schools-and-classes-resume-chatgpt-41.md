![ChatGPT registers huge surge in traffic as schools (and classes) resume — ChatGPT 4.1 mini helps OpenAI to record highs as daily token generation reaches a staggering 78 billion](/assets/images/chatgpt-registers-huge-surge-in-traffic-as-schools-and-classes-resume-chatgpt-41-hero.webp)
As schools kicked off in September 2025, ChatGPT's traffic exploded to a record 78.3 billion daily tokens, largely fueled by students diving back into AI for homework hacks and study aids—no big surprise, given how summer slumps dropped usage to just 36.7 billion back in June. OpenRouter's data paints a clear picture of this academic rhythm, with ChatGPT 4.1 Mini leading the charge at 26.9 billion tokens, outpacing even the shiny new GPT-5 at 18.7 billion, showing how refined models are keeping users hooked. For SMBs and MSPs eyeing AI tools, this surge highlights the growing reliance on chatbots for productivity, but it's a reminder to guide your teams on ethical use to avoid turning students—or your staff—into over-reliant button-pushers. While the debate rages on about balancing AI in education, savvy small-business owners can leverage similar tech to streamline operations, like automating content creation, without letting it replace critical thinking.
