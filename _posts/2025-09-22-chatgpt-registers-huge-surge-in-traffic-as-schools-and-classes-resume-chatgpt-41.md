![ChatGPT registers huge surge in traffic as schools (and classes) resume — ChatGPT 4.1 mini helps OpenAI to record highs as daily token generation reaches a staggering 78 billion](/assets/images/chatgpt-registers-huge-surge-in-traffic-as-schools-and-classes-resume-chatgpt-41-hero.webp)
As schools kicked off in September 2025, ChatGPT's traffic exploded to a record 78.3 billion daily tokens—up from a sluggish 36.7 billion during summer break—proving once again that students are the AI's biggest fanbase, much like how coffee shops thrive on caffeine-craving commuters. OpenRouter's data, tracking over 2.5 million users, spotlights ChatGPT 4.1 Mini as the star performer with 26.9 billion tokens, outpacing the shiny new GPT-5 at 18.7 billion, which might make you wonder if OpenAI's latest isn't quite stealing the show yet. This seasonal surge underscores how AI tools are becoming essential for students tackling writing, research, and study tasks, but it's a wake-up call for SMBs and MSPs to harness similar tech for boosting productivity without letting it turn into a crutch. If you're running a small business, think of this as a nudge to integrate affordable AI like ChatGPT's mini models into your workflow for smarter operations, all while keeping an eye on ethical use to avoid the pitfalls of over-reliance.
