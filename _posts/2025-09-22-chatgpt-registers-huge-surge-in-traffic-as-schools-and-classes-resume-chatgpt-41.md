ChatGPT's traffic just hit a massive high of 78.3 billion tokens on September 18, 2025, thanks to students flooding back to class after a lazy summer break—proving once again that AI's biggest fans wear backpacks. OpenRouter's data shows ChatGPT 4.1 Mini leading the charge with 26.9 billion tokens, outpacing even the shiny new GPT-5 at 18.7 billion, which highlights how optimized models are keeping OpenAI's engine humming for everyday tasks. If you're an SMB or MSP dealing with seasonal swings in your own tech tools, this spike underscores the predictable ebb and flow of AI adoption, driven largely by education demands like essay writing and quick research hacks. While it's tempting to roll your eyes at kids leaning on chatbots for homework, savvy business owners can take a page from this playbook by integrating similar AI efficiencies to boost productivity without replacing the human touch—after all, teaching your team to wield these tools responsibly could be the smart move to stay ahead.
