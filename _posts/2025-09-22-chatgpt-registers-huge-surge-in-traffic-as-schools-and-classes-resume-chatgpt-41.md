As schools kicked off in September 2025, ChatGPT's traffic exploded to a record 78.3 billion daily tokens, highlighting how student demand drives AI usage spikes while summer breaks cause predictable lulls—think of it as the academic calendar giving OpenAI's servers a seasonal workout. OpenRouter's data paints a clear picture, with ChatGPT 4.1 Mini leading the charge at 26.9 billion tokens, outpacing even the shiny new GPT-5 at 18.7 billion, proving that efficient, budget-friendly models are winning the day for everyday users. For tech-curious SMBs and MSPs, this surge underscores the growing reliance on AI for tasks like content creation and research, but don't just jump in blindly—consider integrating tools like these to boost productivity without overwhelming your team's workflow. While students are gobbling up these resources for homework hacks, savvy small-business owners can stay ahead by training staff on ethical AI use, turning potential distractions into real efficiency gains before the next school year hits.
