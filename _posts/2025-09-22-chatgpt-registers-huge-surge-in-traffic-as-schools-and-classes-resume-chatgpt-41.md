![ChatGPT registers huge surge in traffic as schools (and classes) resume — ChatGPT 4.1 mini helps OpenAI to record highs as daily token generation reaches a staggering 78 billion](/assets/images/chatgpt-registers-huge-surge-in-traffic-as-schools-and-classes-resume-chatgpt-41-hero.webp)
ChatGPT's traffic has skyrocketed to a whopping 78.3 billion tokens daily as students drag themselves back to class in September 2025, proving once again that AI chatbots thrive on exam-season panic—think of it as a digital caffeine hit for procrastinators. OpenRouter's data highlights a stark drop-off during summer breaks, with June dipping to just 36.7 billion tokens, underscoring how academic calendars dictate AI usage patterns that SMBs could mimic for their own tools. Leading the charge is the efficient ChatGPT 4.1 Mini, churning out 26.9 billion tokens and outpacing even the shiny new GPT-5 at 18.7 billion, showing that sometimes the streamlined options pack the real punch for cost-conscious operations. For tech-savvy small-business owners and MSPs eyeing AI adoption, this surge signals opportunities to integrate similar models for content creation or customer support, but remember to guide your team's use responsibly to avoid turning it into a crutch rather than a boost.
