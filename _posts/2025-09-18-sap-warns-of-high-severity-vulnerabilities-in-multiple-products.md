### SAP's Latest Headache: High-Risk Flaws in Key Products SAP has uncovered a bunch of serious vulnerabilities, with CVE-2025-42957 standing out as a nightmare—it's a backdoor that lets attackers with basic access totally hijack systems, no sweat involved. This flaw requires only low-level credentials and can be exploited remotely with minimal effort, earning a sky-high CVSS score of 9.9, which means fraud, data breaches, or ransomware could hit fast and hard. Other affected products include SAP Business One, S/4HANA, and several more, with severity levels from moderate to critical. For SMBs and MSPs relying on these tools for daily operations, this is a wake-up call since even a simple phishing attack could snowball into major downtime, financial losses, or compliance nightmares. Ignoring these risks might seem tempting in a busy schedule, but quick patching is essential to keep your business tech from becoming an easy target for cybercriminals. Takeaways: - Quick patches prevent easy system takeovers by hackers.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Low-cred exploits raise insider threat risks for small teams.
- Unpatched flaws could spike ransomware costs for MSPs.
- Check SAP's security page for full vulnerability details.
