### SAP's Latest Security Slip-Ups: High-Risk Flaws in Key Products SAP has uncovered a bunch of nasty vulnerabilities across its software lineup, with the big one being CVE-2025-42957—a flaw that lets attackers with just basic access totally hijack systems with little hassle. This backdoor essentially hands over the keys to the kingdom, potentially exposing sensitive data and disrupting operations without needing any user buy-in. Other hits include risks in products like SAP Business One and S/4HANA, with severity scores up to 9.9, meaning they're as bad as they get. For SMBs and MSPs relying on these tools for daily operations, this is a wake-up call since a breach could mean lost revenue, compliance headaches, or even ransomware nightmares that hit small teams hardest. Ignoring these issues might seem tempting to save time, but quick fixes are crucial to keep your tech stack from turning into a hacker's playground. Takeaways: - Quick exploits could devastate SMB data with minimal attacker effort.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Patching high-severity flaws prevents costly downtime and breaches.
- Basic access risks mean phishing could lead to full takeovers.
- Unpatched systems raise fraud and espionage threats for MSPs.
