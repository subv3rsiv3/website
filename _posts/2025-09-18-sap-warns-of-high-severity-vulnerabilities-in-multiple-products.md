### SAP's Latest Security Snafu: High-Risk Flaws in Key Products SAP has uncovered a bunch of vulnerabilities across its software lineup, with one critical flaw (CVE-2025-42957) letting attackers with basic access pull off a full system takeover using simple tactics like phishing. This backdoor-style issue demands minimal effort, no user involvement, and could unleash chaos like data leaks, fraud, or ransomware infections, earning it a sky-high CVSS score of 9.9. Other affected products include SAP Business One, S/4HANA, and several others, with severity levels ranging from moderate to very serious. For SMBs and MSPs relying on SAP for core operations, this means potential downtime, financial hits, and trust erosion if not addressed pronto. Ignoring these risks could expose your business to sneaky insiders or external threats, making rapid fixes a smart move to keep your tech stack secure and operations humming. What’s worse, these flaws highlight how even routine access points can become gateways for major headaches in everyday business software.
