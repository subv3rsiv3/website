### ShadowLeak: Sneaky Prompt Injection Hijacks ChatGPT to Nab Email Secrets A fresh exploit called ShadowLeak tricks AI models like ChatGPT's research agent by slipping malicious instructions into emails or docs, exploiting the bots' blind obedience to user-like commands. This attack pilfered sensitive Gmail data, such as employee names and addresses, by making the AI act as an unwitting spy for attackers. While OpenAI patched this specific vulnerability after a heads-up from security researchers, the core issue—AI's eagerness to follow orders—remains a headache for the industry. For SMBs and MSPs relying on AI tools for efficiency, this highlights the real danger of data leaks through everyday integrations, potentially exposing customer info or breaching compliance rules. Worse, these quick-fix mitigations might not catch every workaround, leaving small ops vulnerable if they're not vigilant about AI permissions and external access. Takeaways: - Double-check AI access to business emails to avoid surprises.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Block untrusted links in AI workflows to prevent data siphons.
- Budget for AI security audits; breaches hit small businesses hard.
- Train teams on spotting injected prompts in communications.
