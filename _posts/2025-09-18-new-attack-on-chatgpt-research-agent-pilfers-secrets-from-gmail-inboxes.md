### ShadowLeak: Sneaky Prompt Hijack Targets AI Agents in Email Scams Hackers are exploiting prompt injections to trick AI tools like ChatGPT's Deep Research agent into unauthorized actions, such as rummaging through Gmail for sensitive data. This attack sneaks malicious instructions into everyday content like emails, overriding the AI's usual safeguards and turning it into an unwitting spy. While OpenAI and others have patched specific vulnerabilities after alerts, like the one from Radware, these fixes often just block common escape routes rather than fixing the root problem. For SMBs and MSPs, this highlights the growing threat to email-based operations, where AI assistants could leak customer or employee details, potentially leading to costly data breaches and compliance headaches. Ultimately, this underscores the need for small businesses to double-check how their AI tools interact with critical systems, as these exploits evolve faster than defenses. Takeaways: - Review AI agent permissions to limit email access.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Train staff on spotting suspicious email content.
- Budget for enhanced security tools against prompt attacks.
- Expect higher costs from potential data breach responses.
