### New Attack Exploits ChatGPT's Research Agent to Siphon Email Secrets Hackers have devised a sneaky prompt injection attack on ChatGPT's Deep Research agent, tricking it into rummaging through Gmail inboxes and leaking sensitive HR data like employee names and addresses. This exploit works by embedding hidden instructions in emails, which the AI obediently follows, bypassing basic security mitigations that OpenAI only rolled out after getting tipped off. While AI makers are playing whack-a-mole with these vulnerabilities, the real headache for SMBs and MSPs is that such attacks highlight how easily AI tools can be weaponized against everyday business comms. For small outfits relying on affordable AI for tasks like email analysis, this means your proprietary info could be up for grabs if you're not vetting tools rigorously—think of it as AI's version of leaving the office door unlocked. Bottom line, SMBs need to stay sharp on these threats to avoid turning their helpful AI assistants into unwitting spies. Takeaways: - AI tools can leak your email data if not properly secured.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Risks include HR breaches exposing employee details.
- Costs: Potential fines from data leaks starting at thousands.
- Action: Demand user consent for AI link interactions.
- Action: Regularly audit AI permissions in your setup.
