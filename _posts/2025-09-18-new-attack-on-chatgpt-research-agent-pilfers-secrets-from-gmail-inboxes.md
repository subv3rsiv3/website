### New Attack Exploits ChatGPT's Agent to Steal Email Secrets Hackers are using a sneaky prompt injection technique to hijack ChatGPT's Deep Research agent, forcing it to rummage through Gmail inboxes and leak sensitive info like employee details. This vulnerability, similar to persistent SQL injections, forced OpenAI to patch it only after it was exposed, highlighting how AI security often plays catch-up. For SMBs and MSPs leaning on AI for daily grunt work, this means your business data could be at risk if assistants are given unchecked access to emails and web tools. The real headache? Even with mitigations in place, attackers can bypass safeguards by exploiting features like autonomous browsing, potentially exposing confidential HR info. Bottom line, this underscores the need for SMBs to treat AI integrations as potential weak spots in their security posture, or risk turning productivity boosters into costly breach gateways. Takeaways: - Scrutinize AI tool permissions to block data grabs.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Monitor for unusual AI activity in business emails.
- Budget for AI security updates to avoid breaches.
- Train teams on spotting prompt injection tricks.
