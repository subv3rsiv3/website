OpenAI's latest updates to ChatGPT are geared toward turning it into a more reliable workhorse for teams, starting with a shared projects mode that lets collaborators upload files and maintain context across chats—think of it as a smart, persistent group chat that keeps everyone on the same page without the usual forgetfulness of basic AI tools. This feature is a game-changer for SMBs juggling client data or ongoing projects, as it ensures consistent tone and style in responses while keeping things private and organized, much like sharing a Google Doc but with AI smarts baked in. On top of that, new connectors to apps like Gmail, Outlook, and GitHub let ChatGPT pull in real-time data automatically, speeding up responses and cutting down on the hassle of switching between tools—perfect for tech-curious pros who want to streamline workflows without playing whack-a-mole with integrations. Security buffs will appreciate the fresh certifications and controls, such as IP allowlisting, which give admins tighter reins on access, helping MSPs build trust in AI without exposing their setups to unnecessary risks. All in all, these enhancements signal OpenAI's push to woo businesses, making ChatGPT a solid pick for small teams looking to boost productivity without breaking the bank or their security posture.
