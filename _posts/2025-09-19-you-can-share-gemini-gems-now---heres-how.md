Google's Gemini AI has rolled out a handy update, letting users share their custom Gems—basically, tailored AI assistants—with teams or family via a simple click. This works just like sharing Google Docs, where you generate a link or email invite, keeping access controlled to avoid mishaps. For SMBs and MSPs, this means ditching repetitive instructions for AI tasks, freeing up time for actual business stuff instead of handholding bots. It's a no-brainer productivity booster, especially if your crew juggles routine queries or data wrangling that these Gems can handle on autopilot. And since it's integrated with familiar Google tools, even tech-curious small-business owners can jump in without a steep learning curve, making AI more accessible for everyday operations. Takeaways: - Boost team efficiency by sharing AI setups for common tasks. - Watch for data leaks when granting Gem access. - Start small with Gems to cut down on routine instructions.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Factor in setup time before scaling AI sharing.
