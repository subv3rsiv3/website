Google Gemini's Sharing Upgrade for Custom AI Helpers Google's Gemini platform now lets you share custom AI assistants, called Gems, with your team or family, streamlining repetitive tasks without starting from scratch each time. These Gems act as tailored bots that remember your instructions, making them perfect for automating workflows like data cleaning or list compilation. The sharing process is straightforward, mirroring how you handle Google Docs—just hit share, add emails, or generate a link with access controls. For SMBs and MSPs, this means ditching manual handoffs and boosting efficiency, especially when collaborating on tech projects or client services. It's a sneaky productivity win, turning your AI setups into reusable team assets that cut down on busywork and errors. Takeaways: - Boost team productivity by sharing AI tools effortlessly.

**Key takeaways**
- Guard against data leaks when distributing Gems.
- Integrate Gems into daily ops for quick setup savings.
- Experiment with Gems to automate routine SMB tasks.
