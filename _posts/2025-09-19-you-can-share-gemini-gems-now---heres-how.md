Google Gemini Gems: Share Your Custom AI Helpers Effortlessly Google's Gemini Gems let you create tailored AI assistants for repetitive tasks, like a smart intern that remembers your instructions without nagging. Now, with a recent update, sharing these Gems is as straightforward as passing around a Google Doc— just hit share and send a link or email. This means small businesses and MSPs can collaborate on AI tools without reinventing the wheel for every team member, saving precious time on setup. For SMBs, this feature could supercharge productivity by letting non-techies access customized AI for things like inventory tracking or client queries, without the hassle of full-blown integrations. Of course, it's a cheeky way for Google to hook more users into their ecosystem, but hey, if it streamlines your operations, who's complaining? And since it's built on familiar tools, even tech-curious owners can jump in without a steep learning curve. Takeaways: - Share Gems to streamline team workflows instantly.

**Key takeaways**
- Guard shared links to avoid unintended data exposure.
- Leverage free tools to reduce custom AI development costs.
- Test Gems for everyday SMB tasks like list compilation.
