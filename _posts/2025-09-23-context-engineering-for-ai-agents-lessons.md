When building AI agents like those at Manus, it's smarter to leverage in-context learning from beefy LLMs rather than wasting weeks fine-tuning models from scratch—trust me, that's a rookie mistake that'll kill your startup's momentum. Focus on boosting KV-cache hit rates to slash latency and costs by keeping prompts stable, contexts append-only, and avoiding dumb tweaks like timestamp fiddling; this way, SMBs can run agents efficiently without breaking the bank. Instead of dynamically messing with tool sets, which tanks cache and confuses models, use logit masking to guide action selection smartly, ensuring your agent stays on track without invalidating previous context. Treat the file system as your agent's external brain for handling massive contexts, recite key objectives to keep attention sharp, and leave errors in the mix so the model learns from its screw-ups rather than repeating them. Finally, ditch rigid few-shot prompting to prevent your agent from getting stuck in a rut, and remember, nailing context engineering means faster iterations and more reliable AI for tech pros and small biz owners pushing the envelope.
