Even the most swamped execs in SMBs and MSPs can squeeze real value from volunteering in non-executive roles, like joining boards or mentoring, without derailing their day jobs. You'll sharpen your strategic thinking by viewing organizations from an outsider's lens, helping you spot big-picture opportunities that your own biz might be missing. Building a broader network through these gigs exposes you to fresh leadership styles and industry insights, almost like a crash course MBA on the cheap. Plus, guiding up-and-comers or contributing to community tech efforts not only hones your skills but also boosts your feel-good factor, turning you into a more adaptable leader. And hey, if you're an MSP owner, this is a smart way to fill skill gaps in your team while keeping your operations secure and innovative.
