### FCC Under Fire for Alleged Media Meddling The FCC is facing backlash for what critics call censorship, particularly after threats to pull Jimmy Kimmel from ABC amid claims of political bias in his commentary. This stems from the Trump administration's push to influence media coverage, with FCC Chair Brendan Carr hinting at regulatory actions like suspensions or license reviews for networks. Former officials and advocacy groups are slamming this as a dangerous erosion of First Amendment protections, especially since the FCC oversees broadcast licenses for major outlets. For SMBs and MSPs, this highlights the growing risks of government overreach in content regulation, potentially affecting how small businesses manage online presence, partnerships with media, or even their own tech-driven communications strategies. It underscores the need for vigilance in a landscape where regulatory threats could disrupt digital ecosystems and force compliance burdens on resource-strapped operations. Takeaways: - Watch for stricter content rules impacting your online marketing.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Prep for potential compliance costs from biased content claims.
- Boost internal policies to avoid regulatory scrutiny on media ties.
- Evaluate partnerships with broadcasters for hidden risks.
