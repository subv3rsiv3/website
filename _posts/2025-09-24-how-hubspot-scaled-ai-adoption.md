![How HubSpot Scaled AI Adoption](/assets/images/how-hubspot-scaled-ai-adoption-hero.webp)
HubSpot's journey with AI coding tools, like GitHub Copilot, is a masterclass in ditching the hype and getting practical results—starting from a skeptical pilot in 2023, backed by executive nudges, and scaling to nearly universal adoption across their engineering crew. They smartly ran large-scale tests, focused on training and metrics to bust biases, and saw solid productivity boosts that justified the modest costs, even if it didn't live up to the wild promises floating around. To ramp up further, they formed a dedicated AI team to handle everything from tool customization to community building, eventually dropping restrictions and using data and peer stories to win over the skeptics. For SMBs and MSPs looking to follow suit, prioritize top-level buy-in, measure real impacts on your workflows, and tailor AI to your tech stack to avoid costly missteps. In the end, this approach not only smoothed the path to widespread use but also unlocked advanced features, proving that a measured, team-focused strategy can turn AI from a nice-to-have into a game-changer for your ops.
