Google's Gemini Gems let you whip up specialized AI chatbots for tasks like meal planning or kid-scheduling without endlessly re-explaining your needs, and now you can easily share these custom gems with colleagues or clients by simply hitting the Share button and sending a link—just like firing off a Google Doc. This means recipients can view, duplicate, or even tweak your AI helper with your okay, making collaboration smoother and cutting down on the tedious back-and-forth that small businesses often deal with when deploying AI tools. It's a smart riff on ChatGPT's custom GPTs but with the bonus of Google Drive's familiar permissions, so MSPs and SMBs can manage sharing without learning a new system, though admins in Google Workspace get extra controls to lock things down. Of course, there are caveats—like not sharing gems with sensitive data—but overall, this feature turns your AI creations into reusable team assets, letting you prompt less and innovate more without the usual hassle. While it won't solve every AI sharing snag, it's a solid step forward for tech-savvy pros who want to streamline workflows and maybe even discover clever uses from others' setups.
