Google's Gemini Gems let you whip up specialized AI chatbots for tasks like managing a toddler's sleep or quick meal planning, and now you can share these custom helpers with friends or colleagues by simply clicking a "Share" button, much like firing off a Google Doc. This setup turns your AI creations into portable, collaborative tools that others can view, duplicate, or even tweak with your okay, slashing the hassle of re-explaining prompts and letting you focus on getting stuff done. For SMBs and MSPs, it's a game-changer since it integrates seamlessly with Google Workspace, where admins can slap on sharing restrictions to keep things secure, mirroring familiar Drive policies so no one has to learn a new system. Of course, there are caveats—like not sharing Gems with sensitive data—but overall, this feature edges out rivals like ChatGPT's custom GPTs by leveraging the Drive ecosystem, making it easier for tech-savvy pros to collaborate without reinventing the wheel each time.
