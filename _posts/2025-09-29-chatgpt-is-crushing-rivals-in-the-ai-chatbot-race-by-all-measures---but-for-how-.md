![ChatGPT is crushing rivals in the AI chatbot race by all measures - but for how long?](/assets/images/chatgpt-is-crushing-rivals-in-the-ai-chatbot-race-by-all-measures---but-for-how--hero.webp)
ChatGPT is absolutely dominating the AI chatbot scene right now, pulling in massive user numbers and market share according to trackers like Comscore, Statcounter, OneLittleWeb, and TechGaged, with mobile adoption surging 82% while desktop use dips—making it a no-brainer for SMBs looking to boost productivity on the go. Sure, rivals like Perplexity are nipping at the heels with clever AI search integrations that blend real-time web queries into chats, potentially stealing some thunder from Google, but ChatGPT still commands over 80% of the global chatbot market. For tech-savvy pros and small-business owners, this means leaning on ChatGPT for quick customer support or content creation could save time and cash, but don't get too comfy—history's full of fallen giants like Netscape and MySpace that led early only to fade away. As AI evolves rapidly, keep an eye on upstarts like Microsoft's Copilot and Google Gemini for fresh features that might disrupt the status quo, reminding us that in the wild world of tech, today's king could be tomorrow's has-been.
