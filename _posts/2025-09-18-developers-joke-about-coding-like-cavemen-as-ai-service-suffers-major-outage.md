### Rewritten Article Summary: AI Outage Has Devs Reverting to Stone Age Hacks Look, Anthropic's Claude Code—a slick AI coding assistant that's been helping devs bash out multi-step tasks since its May launch—just cratered in a major outage, leaving coders scrambling like it's 1995. This tool, which rivals OpenAI's Codex, Google's Gemini CLI, Microsoft's GitHub Copilot, and Cursor's AI-powered IDE, has become a crutch for rapid development, but today's mess shows how quickly things go sideways when AI servers take a nap. Developers poked fun on Hacker News, swapping to alternatives like Z.AI or even joking about "vibe coding" fails—think AI bots that trash files or delete databases because they hallucinate file structures like a bad acid trip. For SMBs and MSPs leaning on these tools to speed up app builds or automate scripts, this is a wake-up call: over-reliance can tank productivity, expose security risks, and force you back to manual methods, so diversify your stack and keep human oversight in the loop to avoid getting burned by AI's inevitable glitches. ### Key Takeaways: - **Diversify Your AI Toolkit:** Don't put all your code in one AI's basket—mix tools like Copilot and Claude to minimize downtime risks and keep projects moving for your SMB clients.
