### AI Coding Tools Hit a Snag: Outage Exposes Developer Dependencies Anthropic's Claude Code, a popular AI-driven coding assistant for handling complex tasks in codebases, recently went down, sparking widespread chatter among developers. This event highlights the rapid adoption of AI tools like Claude Code, which competes with offerings from OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft, as go-to aids for speeding up software creation. For SMBs and MSPs leaning on these tools to boost efficiency in app development or IT services, the outage underscores the vulnerability of relying on external AI services that can suddenly fail. It also serves as a humorous nudge for tech pros to remember their roots—jokes about "caveman coding" remind us that over-dependence on AI might lead to blind spots, like generating flawed code without full understanding. Ultimately, this disruption emphasizes the need for SMBs and MSPs to weigh AI's productivity gains against the real risks of downtime and errors in their operations. Takeaways: - Diversify AI tools to avoid full shutdowns.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Verify all AI code to prevent hidden errors.
- Budget for outage downtime in operations.
- Train staff on manual coding as a backup.
- Assess AI dependency before full adoption.
