### Data Breaches: A Wake-Up Call for the Little Guys Data breaches have plagued businesses since the 1980s, starting with simple password leaks like the TRW incident that exposed millions of credit histories via a shared bulletin board. Over time, as the internet and IoT exploded, attacks evolved into sophisticated operations targeting everything from social media accounts to government databases, as seen in the LinkedIn hack and major retail breaches at Target and Home Depot. These incidents highlight how attackers exploit weak points like reused passwords or vulnerable payment systems to steal personal data, which then fuels a thriving black market. For SMBs and MSPs, this means your small-scale operations are prime targets for cybercriminals, potentially leading to hefty fines, lost customer trust, and operational disruptions that could sink your business. Ignoring these lessons is like leaving your front door wide open in a bad neighborhood—it's not if, but when, you'll get hit, so beefing up security isn't just smart, it's survival. Takeaways: - Regularly audit passwords to prevent reuse across accounts.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Invest in basic encryption to protect customer data from leaks.
- Train staff on phishing risks to avoid easy breaches.
- Budget for breach response plans; downtime costs add up fast.
