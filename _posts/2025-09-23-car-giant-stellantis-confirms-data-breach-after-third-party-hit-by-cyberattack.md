![Car giant Stellantis confirms data breach after third-party hit by cyberattack](/assets/images/car-giant-stellantis-confirms-data-breach-after-third-party-hit-by-cyberattack-hero.webp)
Stellantis, the massive automaker behind brands like Jeep and Dodge, has admitted to a data breach that hit a third-party platform handling its North American customer services—blame it on the notorious ShinyHunters group, who's been on a rampage stealing Salesforce-related records from big names. While the breach only exposed basic contact info and spared any juicy financial details, it's a stark reminder that even giants rely on external vendors who can be soft targets. This isn't an isolated incident, as ShinyHunters reportedly snagged over 18 million records, affecting other tech players like Google and Cloudflare, highlighting how interconnected systems can drag everyone down. For SMBs and MSPs juggling similar setups, it's a wake-up call to audit your third-party dependencies and train staff to spot phishing scams—don't click that suspicious link, folks, and consider beefing up your multi-factor authentication to stay one step ahead of these digital pests.
