### Ex-WhatsApp Security Chief Sues Meta, Calling Their Culture a "Cult"—What SMBs and MSPs Need to Know Look, even tech behemoths like Meta aren't immune to epic security fails: Former WhatsApp security boss Waqar Baig is suing the company, claiming its cult-like culture stifles any pushback on shady practices, like engineers having free rein over user data without proper oversight. In his complaint, Baig highlights glaring issues, including Meta's failure to track user data inventories, monitor access, or detect breaches—stuff that's basic table stakes under privacy laws in California, the EU, and FTC agreements—leading to daily hacks affecting up to 400,000 users and rampant data scraping for scams. This whistleblower saga underscores how retaliation against security watchdogs can exacerbate problems, with Baig alleging Meta faked reports to cover up risks. For SMBs and MSPs, this is a stark reminder to lock down your data governance pronto, as sloppy practices could invite lawsuits, regulatory smackdowns, and customer trust meltdowns—think of it as a free lesson from Meta's mess. Ultimately, building a transparent culture that encourages reporting vulnerabilities isn't just smart; it's your best defense against turning into the next headline. ### Key Takeaways for SMBs and MSPs: - **Audit your data access now**: Ensure you have systems to inventory, monitor, and secure user data—don't wait for a breach to expose your blind spots, like Meta did.
