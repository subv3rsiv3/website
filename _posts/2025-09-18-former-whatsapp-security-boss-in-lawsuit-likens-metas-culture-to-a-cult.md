### Former WhatsApp Security Chief Sues Meta Over "Cult-Like" Culture and Data Blunders A former WhatsApp security leader has filed a lawsuit against Meta, accusing the company of fostering a rigid, unquestioning culture that ignored serious data security flaws, like improper access to user info and a lack of basic monitoring tools. This mess allegedly led to massive account hacks—up to 400,000 users locked out daily—and non-compliance with privacy laws in places like the EU and California. The exec claims he raised red flags for years, only to face retaliation and see reports fudged to hide risks. For SMBs and MSPs, this saga underscores the perils of skimping on data governance, as even big players like Meta can stumble into costly breaches that erode trust and invite regulatory smackdowns. It’s a wake-up call to prioritize whistleblower protections and robust security practices to avoid similar headaches in your own operations. Takeaways: - Bolster data monitoring to catch breaches early.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Enforce whistleblower policies to prevent retaliation.
- Audit data storage regularly to meet compliance.
- Invest in anti-scraping tools to protect user info.
