### Former WhatsApp Security Chief Sues Meta Over "Cult-Like" Culture and Data Blunders A former WhatsApp security leader has filed a lawsuit against Meta, claiming the company's rigid, unquestioning culture stifles security improvements and led to widespread data mishandling. He alleges Meta ignored basic data governance essentials, like tracking user data storage and monitoring access, which violated privacy laws in regions like the EU and California. This whistleblower also accused Meta of retaliating against him for raising red flags on issues such as unchecked data scraping and massive daily account hacks affecting hundreds of thousands of users. For SMBs and MSPs, this saga highlights the perils of skimping on security protocols, as similar oversights could expose small operations to hefty fines, reputational damage, and legal battles. Ultimately, it serves as a wake-up call for tech-curious businesses to foster a more open culture around security concerns, ensuring they're not just copying big tech's bad habits but building resilient defenses against everyday threats. Takeaways: - Poor data tracking risks massive breaches and fines for SMBs.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Ignoring security culture invites internal retaliation and leaks.
- Quick data inventory checks can prevent costly compliance issues.
- Bolster monitoring to combat daily hacks like WhatsApp's woes.
