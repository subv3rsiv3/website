### Former WhatsApp Security Chief Sues Meta, Calling Out "Cult-Like" Culture A former WhatsApp security executive has filed a lawsuit against Meta, alleging the company fosters a rigid, unquestioning culture that stifles challenges to poor data practices—essentially treating dissent like heresy. He claims Meta ignored critical issues, such as unauthorized access to user data, inadequate tracking of data storage, and a lack of systems to monitor breaches or comply with global privacy laws. This led to massive vulnerabilities, including daily hacks affecting hundreds of thousands of users and unchecked data scraping for scams. For SMBs and MSPs, this saga underscores the perils of skimping on data governance, as similar oversights could trigger hefty fines, customer distrust, and regulatory scrutiny that small outfits can ill afford. Ultimately, it serves as a wake-up call for tech pros to prioritize robust security frameworks to avoid the kind of blowback Meta's facing. Takeaways: - Prioritize data inventory to dodge privacy law violations.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Implement breach monitoring to prevent daily user risks.
- Foster open reporting culture to avoid retaliation pitfalls.
- Budget for security upgrades to counter scraping threats.
