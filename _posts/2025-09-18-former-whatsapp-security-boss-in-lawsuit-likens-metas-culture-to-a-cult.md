### Former WhatsApp Security Chief Sues Meta, Calling Out a Toxic Culture A former WhatsApp security leader has filed a lawsuit against Meta, accusing the company of fostering a cult-like environment where challenging upper management on data issues is taboo. This stems from alleged failures in data governance, including lax controls on user data access, no proper inventory or tracking systems, and inability to spot breaches—violating key privacy laws and FTC agreements. Despite raising alarms to top execs like Zuckerberg, the whistleblower claims Meta retaliated and even faked security reports to hide risks. For SMBs and MSPs, this saga underscores the dangers of weak internal security cultures, as similar oversights could expose small operations to hefty fines, data leaks, and trust erosion in a hyper-regulated digital world. Ultimately, it serves as a wake-up call for tech pros to prioritize robust data practices before problems escalate. Takeaways: - Ensure data access logs to avoid undetected breaches.

        **Key takeaways**
        - Foster open whistleblower policies to catch risks early.
- Regularly audit data storage for compliance and costs.
- Train teams on challenging hierarchy without fear.
