Generative AI isn't the job-killing monster it's often made out to be—according to Indeed's latest report, it's more about supercharging workflows than wiping out positions outright, with 26% of jobs potentially getting a major overhaul from tools like GPT-4.1 and Claude Sonnet 4. Cognitive-heavy roles, such as software dev, are prime for AI integration, while grunt-work jobs like nursing remain largely untouched, giving SMBs a chance to boost efficiency without the panic. That said, only a tiny fraction (0.7%) of skills are on the chopping block for full automation, so stop fretting about the AI apocalypse and start experimenting with models that fit your biz. For MSPs and small outfits, the key is picking the right AI for your specific needs—think tailored implementations over blanket rollouts—to avoid those infamous 95% failure rates from rushed adoptions, ultimately making your team smarter, not smaller.
