Baker University's recent data breach, stemming from a December 2024 intrusion, exposed sensitive info like Social Security numbers, financial details, and health records for over 53,000 folks—mostly students, staff, and affiliates—after hackers lurked in their systems for nearly three weeks. This isn't just a campus headache; it's a stark reminder that even established institutions can get caught flat-footed by basic network vulnerabilities, much like the voice phishing attacks hitting Harvard and Princeton lately. While Baker's team is playing catch-up with external cyber experts, rebuilding compromised platforms, and offering free credit monitoring, they've got no proof of fraud yet—but that's cold comfort for those whose data got swiped. SMBs and MSPs, take note: this mess highlights why you need airtight identity and access management (IAM) right now, so don't wait for your own outage to lock down user permissions and patch those zero-day holes before the next cyber gang comes knocking.
