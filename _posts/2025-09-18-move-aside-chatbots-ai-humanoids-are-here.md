### AI in Everyday Gizmos: From Smart Pet Doors to High-Tech Apparel In a recent tech chat, experts discussed practical AI applications beyond flashy chatbots, spotlighting a clever cat flap that uses computer vision to scan for "contraband" like prey in your pet's mouth and block entry with notifications. This ties into broader AI trends for smart homes, showing how affordable tech can automate mundane tasks and enhance security. For SMBs and MSPs, these innovations highlight opportunities to integrate AI into consumer products, potentially cutting operational costs or creating new service offerings in IoT and home automation. Meanwhile, the conversation veered into wearable tech, like merino wool T-shirts that wick moisture and fight odors, illustrating how materials tech can improve comfort for active lifestyles—relevant for businesses in outdoor or field services. Ultimately, this underscores why SMBs should explore AI not just for big projects, but for everyday solutions that boost efficiency and customer appeal.
