Gemini Gems: Sharing Your Custom AI Sidekicks Made Easy Google's Gemini Gems let you build specialized AI chatbots for tasks like meal planning or scheduling, saving you from repeating instructions every time. Now, with a simple share button, you can link your Gems to colleagues or clients, much like sharing a Google Doc, letting them view, copy, or modify with your okay. This update boosts collaboration by turning your AI creations into reusable resources, cutting down on the hassle of recreating prompts. For SMBs and MSPs, this means faster team workflows and less time fiddling with AI setups, especially if you're already in the Google ecosystem. And with admin controls in Google Workspace, you can lock down sharing to keep things secure, making it a no-brainer for small teams managing tech on a budget. - Streamline SMB operations by sharing AI tools without rebuilding them. - Watch for data risks when sharing Gems with sensitive info.

**Key takeaways**
- Leverage existing Google Drive policies to control access quickly.
- Reduce training costs by distributing pre-configured AI helpers.
- Test shared Gems internally to avoid unintended exposure.
