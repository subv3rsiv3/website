Google's Gemini Gems, those handy customizable AI assistants, just got a nifty upgrade: you can now share them with your team or family as easily as passing around a Google Doc. Simply fire up your Gem manager, hit the share button, and you'll get a familiar pop-up to add emails or generate a link—don't forget to lock it down so only the right folks can access it and avoid any unintended AI mishaps. For SMBs and MSPs juggling tight budgets, this means cranking up productivity without reinventing the wheel every time, like turning a shared Gem into a smart grocery list compiler or a data scrubber that saves hours. Setting up a Gem is straightforward—feed it detailed instructions and context, almost like briefing an eager intern, and you can even use Gemini itself to fine-tune those prompts for maximum efficiency. If you're new to this, think of it as a low-cost way to automate repetitive tasks and keep your small business humming along without the headache.
