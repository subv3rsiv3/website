DigiCert is snapping up Valimail to supercharge its DigiCert ONE platform with advanced zero-trust email authentication, directly tackling the rampant phishing and spoofing threats that plague businesses worldwide through robust DMARC enforcement. This move integrates Valimail's expertise in verifying senders and blocking fakes, potentially making features like BIMI logos and Verified Mark Certificates a standard in inboxes, which could simplify email security for SMBs without breaking the bank. As phishing exploits human slip-ups more than ever, this acquisition aims to unify digital trust across websites, devices, and email, giving MSPs a one-stop shop to scale DMARC protections and fend off evolving cyber attacks. While it's a smart play to grab a slice of the massive $4 billion-plus DMARC market, remember that tech alone won't fix everything—SMBs should pair these tools with solid user training to truly lock down their defenses and avoid getting phished.
